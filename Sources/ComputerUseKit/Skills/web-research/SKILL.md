# Web research

- Reach every site with `open_url` — one call per site, then stay in that tab.
- Dismiss cookie banners and sign-in prompts FIRST; they swallow clicks under
  them. Look for "Reject", "Accept", or an X.
- Read what's on screen before scrolling; scroll in increments and stop when
  the answer is visible. Zoom on dense or small text instead of guessing.
- Prefer a site's own search box over scanning long pages.
- Keep track of WHERE each fact came from — the final summary should name the
  sites, not just the claims.
- If a page is paywalled or empty, go back to results and pick another source
  rather than fighting it.
- Finish by writing the summary the user asked for; don't leave them with a
  pile of open pages and no answer.
